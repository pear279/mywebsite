## Design Context

### Users

The primary audience is recruiters, hiring managers, product leaders, and potential collaborators evaluating an AI product manager. Most will scan quickly on desktop, while mobile visitors still need complete access to projects, experience, contact details, and the downloadable resume.

### Brand Personality

Precise, composed, curious. The site should feel like a carefully edited product archive rather than a marketing template: technically fluent, visually literate, and confident without being loud.

### Aesthetic Direction

A restrained editorial portfolio built from warm paper, charcoal surfaces, fine rules, and a single acid-lime accent. The handwritten `pear 279` mark is the memorable human element. References include Karol Ortyl for the hero identity, GreyMac for indexed navigation and sticky project storytelling, and Lenis/GSAP for controlled scroll presence. Avoid oversized headings, generic card grids, excessive glow, and decorative motion without information value.

### Design Principles

1. Keep headings compact; hierarchy should come from spacing, weight, and rhythm before scale.
2. Use motion to explain section changes, active work, and navigation state—not to decorate every element.
3. Preserve one strong idea per section: identity, introduction, chronology, work detail, capabilities, contact.
4. Use the acid accent sparingly for active or actionable states only.
5. Maintain full functionality and legibility with reduced motion, keyboard input, and mobile layouts.
